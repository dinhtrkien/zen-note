# Zen Note — Product Vision

A zen, minimal note-taking app for reading and thinking on iPad.

- PDF on the left; handwritten notes attached to the material on the right.
- Keep reading, summarising, and problem-solving in one calm workspace.
- Prefer simple, predictable interactions over feature-heavy study management.
- Make study data durable, locally accessible, and owned by the user.
- Stay usable offline and free for this personal prototype.

Iteration checklists describe bounded work, not the product's permanent specification.
